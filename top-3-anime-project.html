Welcome to my top 3 anime

  1. Steins;Gate

    One of the best anime I have ever watched.
    The anime skips out on a lot but the Visual Novel is way better

  2. Hyouka

    KyoAni did an amazing job with its animation and the whole "mystery without dead bodies" is actually an interesting take.
    The animation and the story are really good!
    Definitely check it out!

  3. Idaten Jump

    A unique isekai about racing with Idaten Bikes
    My first ever Isekai and I love it.
    The OSTs are sooooo good.
    You should check this out too. It is one of the best osts I have ever heard
